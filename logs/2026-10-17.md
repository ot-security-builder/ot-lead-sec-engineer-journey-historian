### OT SE Quiz Helper (210W-03)
- **Covered:** CISA 210W-03 (Common ICS Components) practice checks and final exam, 90% (18/20), passed.
- **Takeaways:** Field device inputs go from sensors to the controller; outputs go from the controller to valves and pumps. Controllers analyze data and manage the process. Cascade control uses multiple sensors in nested loops. Processes change the chemical or physical properties of materials. SCADA covers wide areas, DCS handles continuous local control. The HMI is the client in SCADA client/server. Topologies: a bus shares one cable, a single ring passes a token clockwise, P2P lets every device talk directly. Fiber is hard to tap. Dedicated lines are owned and private, leased lines come from the phone company, and RF works both on site and over long distances.
