### ICS Unpacked
- **Covered:** Tutored CISA 210W-05 (ICS Cybersecurity Risk), completed with a certificate (0.20 CEUs, 1h45m contact hours), exam 18/20. Concepts: risk = threat × vulnerability × consequence; risk as the likelihood of an undesirable outcome weighed by its severity (OT usually rates likelihood high/medium/low, not exact percentages); protective ideas (segmentation with ingress/egress rules, no externally routable connections, credential management, monitoring plus a Collection Management Framework, incident response); attack vectors (email, internet exposure, removable media); challenges in mitigating vulnerabilities (testing, downtime, post-change monitoring, compensating controls); why IT security tools struggle in ICS (communication speeds, detection, intrusion prevention, resources, compatibility); vendor HMI and engineering software.
- **Takeaways:** If any factor in the risk equation is near zero, risk is near zero. IT protects information, OT protects physical operations. Patching an HMI risks operators losing view and control of the live process. Asset owners depend on vendors for patches and supported security tools.
