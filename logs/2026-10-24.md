- **Takeaways:** Removing internet exposure breaks the attack chain at recon, and weak credentials are the second link. Active scans can crash fragile PLCs, so production OT relies on passive monitoring of a traffic copy (SPAN or tap, PCAP) and keeps active scanning in the lab.
